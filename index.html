Frequently Asked Questions

Is this free to use?

Yes, completely free. No registration, no fees, no limits. Generate as many invoices as you need, forever.

Is my wallet address secure?

Your address is only used locally in your browser to generate the QR code. We never store, transmit, or log any of your data.

Can I use a BNS name instead of an address?

Yes! Enter your .btc name (e.g. flor.btc) and it will automatically resolve to your Stacks address using the Hiro public API.

What tokens are supported?

Currently: STX (native token), sBTC (Bitcoin on Stacks), NOT (Notstrich), WELSH, LEO, and ALEX. More tokens can be added — open source contributions welcome.

How does the QR code payment work?

The QR code encodes your Stacks wallet address. When scanned with any Stacks-compatible wallet (Leather, Xverse), it pre-fills the recipient field for a faster payment experience.

Can I use this on mobile?

Absolutely. The tool is built mobile-first and works seamlessly on all screen sizes. You can generate and share invoices directly from your phone.
